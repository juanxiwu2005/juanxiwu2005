*Project Lead, 6-person team · Fall 2025*

**Approach:** Standardized the combined contract data and calculated forward-looking addressable value for each contract. ### 6 · Askoll EVA: CRM platform & implementation-partner decision
*3-person team · Information Systems Management, Bocconi · Spring 2026*

An e-mobility manufacturer had to choose between **Microsoft Business Central, Salesforce and SAP CRM**, plus an implementation partner for each.

- Modeled 5-year NPV of total cost of ownership and expected benefits for all three options
- Built a weighted scorecard: **strategic fit 33% · implementation feasibility 32% · business capability 20% · partner & governance fit 15%**
